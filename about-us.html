Paul Norman

Paul is a Director of Xenia Management, a high-end residential developer focusing on Belgravia and Knightsbridge that has completed projects with a GDV in excess of £200m.

Tom Pridmore

Partner

After co-founding the DCM Group in 1998, he established its offices in Turkey, India, North Africa, Eastern Europe and the UK. Funders who have stood on the other side of the table.

Beaufort Capital Management UK Limited provides tailored debt and equity solutions to experienced developers, investors and specialist operators across the UK — from £10m to £150m per project.

We offer Senior, Stretched Senior, Mezzanine, Bridge-to-Development and Equity facilities for acquisition, development, repositioning and value-add opportunities across residential, student accommodation, care homes, hotels, industrial and other major real estate sectors.

Our partners and directors have built their careers on both sides of the table — as developers and as financiers. That perspective shapes how we underwrite, structure and support every transaction we back, with flexible structuring, commercial decision-making and certainty of execution from initial enquiry through to completion.

ESG

Beaufort incorporates Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) matters into our investment and business decision-making processes.

We also expect our developer partners to actively incorporate ESG into the management of their business and carefully consider ESG factors in every development they undertake.

We believe a comprehensive ESG framework focussed on sustainable investment will deliver enhanced returns for all stakeholders, improve business performance and most importantly have a positive impact on the wider environment.

Our Commitments

  • We will seek appropriate disclosure on ESG issues by the entities in which we invest.
  • We will work with our clients to reduce the environmental impact and enhance the social impact of their real estate holdings.
  • We will provide thought leadership on sustainable investment.
  • We will regularly review the effectiveness of our sustainable investment policy.
  • We will treat our clients, employees and suppliers ethically, fairly and professionally.
