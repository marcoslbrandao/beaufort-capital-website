Senior Loan

For development, acquisition and value-add projects that need maximum leverage in an efficient capital structure.

Beaufort's Senior and Stretched Senior facilities give experienced developers, investors and specialist operators flexible, tailored funding — with higher leverage available where the scheme supports it.

We support development, acquisition and value-add opportunities across all major real estate sectors throughout England, Scotland and Wales.

With facilities from £10m to £150m, Beaufort provides certainty of execution and bespoke funding solutions for projects ranging from £15m to over £200m GDV.

Mezzanine

Additional capital alongside your senior lender, to optimise leverage and enhance equity returns.

Beaufort's mezzanine facilities are designed to complement senior debt, providing experienced developers, investors and operators with additional capital to optimise leverage and enhance equity returns.

We work seamlessly alongside high street banks, challenger banks and alternative lenders, with established intercreditor arrangements enabling an efficient funding and execution process.

Our mezzanine facilities support development, acquisition and value-add opportunities across all major real estate sectors throughout England, Scotland and Wales, with funding from £3m for projects ranging from £15m to over £200m GDV.

03

Bridge to Development

Capital to progress a site through planning and pre-development work before a full development facility is in place.

Beaufort's Bridge-to-Development finance is designed to unlock sites that require further progression before a full development facility can be secured.

Whether funding planning enhancements, reserved matters, infrastructure and servicing works or other pre-development activities, our facilities provide experienced developers with the capital required to create value and prepare sites for construction.

Available on either a first or second charge basis, Bridge-to-Development finance provides a seamless transition into a senior development facility, helping sponsors maintain momentum and execute projects with confidence.
