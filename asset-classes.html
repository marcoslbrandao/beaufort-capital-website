Retirement Living & Healthcare

What was once a niche sector is now one of the fastest growing real estate sectors in the UK.

Beaufort has significant experience of funding developments right across the acuity spectrum; from retirement living to care, specialist health facilities and supported living.

We have a network of relationships in the sector to enable borrowers to be matched with care providers as well as institutional purchasers, ensuring the best outcome for the project.
